Drawdown = % below the running peak. This is what losing actually felt like, in real time. The flat top means at a new high; deep dips mean deep underwater.
A 50% drawdown needs a 100% gain to recover; a 75% drawdown needs 300%; a 90% drawdown needs 900%. This is why avoiding ruin matters more than maximizing the upside.
